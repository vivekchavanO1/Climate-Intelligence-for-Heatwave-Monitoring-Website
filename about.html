About the project

Use Case KJS-CES-01

Climate Intelligence for Heatwave Monitoring, Prediction, and Early Warning — developed by Vivek Santosh Chavan of Engineering's AI use-case integration framework, in collaboration with the India Meteorological Department (IMD), Mumbai–Pune.

Problem statement

Heatwaves are among the most damaging extreme weather events, growing more frequent and intense with climate change. Their impact on public health, agriculture, and daily life varies sharply by region, making accurate, localized forecasting and early warning essential — yet difficult to deliver at scale.

Background

IMD continuously monitors and forecasts heatwave conditions across India, but the country's diverse climatic zones call for more granular, region-wise intelligence. HeatScope explores how AI-driven spatio-temporal analysis and IoT-based ground observations can sharpen that picture and turn it into stakeholder-ready advisories.

Objectives

  • 01Analyze spatio-temporal temperature patterns and heatwave behaviour through region-wise, seasonal analysis.
  • 02Develop AI-driven models for temperature forecasting, heatwave prediction, hotspot identification, and severity classification.
  • 03Design IoT-enabled Automated Weather Stations for localized monitoring and forecast validation.
  • 04Build visualization, decision-support, and early-warning tools for heatwave advisory dissemination.

Collaborating organization

India Meteorological Department (IMD), Mumbai–Pune

Faculty owner
