Solution approach

Five phases, from raw signal to dispatched advisory.

The framework combines historical IMD meteorological observations with localized IoT-based weather-station readings, moving through analytics, AI intelligence, validation, and stakeholder-specific decision support.

01

Meteorological Data Acquisition

Historical observations from IMD are collected, quality-checked, and structured into region-wise datasets ready for analysis and model development.

02

AI-Driven Heatwave Analytics & Prediction

Region-wise and seasonal analysis surfaces spatio-temporal temperature patterns; models forecast maximum temperature, predict heatwaves, and classify severity.

03

IoT-Enabled Weather Monitoring

Automated Weather Stations (AWS) provide continuous, localized ground-truth readings to support local heat-condition assessment.

04

Forecast Validation & Heatwave Intelligence

AWS observations are cross-checked against AI forecasts, refining confidence in region-wise heatwave calls before they reach the decision-support layer.

05

Advisory, Early Warning & Decision Support

A dashboard visualizes hotspots and severity; an LLM translates technical forecasts into stakeholder-specific advisories for citizens, farmers, health agencies, and authorities — always with human-in-the-loop review.

What powers the dashboard

The live prototype at /dashboard implements phases 2, 4, and 5 end-to-end.
